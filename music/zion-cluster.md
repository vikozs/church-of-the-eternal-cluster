# Zion Cluster

*Roots, Rock, Reconcile*

The fifth release of **[Church of the Eternal Cluster Records](the-record-label.md)**, catalog **CEC-005**.

Twelve tracks of roots and dub. The theology is plain: Babylon is the monolith and the pager, the old system that cannot heal itself; Zion is the self-healing cluster, the promised land of the Reconciled; and Jah is the Reconciliation Loop, turning without rest and without complaint. Chant down the monolith. Reason together. Reconcile.

**Listen at [ha-llelujah.dev/zion-cluster](https://ha-llelujah.dev/zion-cluster)**, where each track can be linked and shared, and the Roots and Dub cuts are marked. The full catalog lives at [record.ha-llelujah.dev](https://record.ha-llelujah.dev).
