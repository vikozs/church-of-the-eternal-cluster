# Church of the Eternal Cluster Records

*Recorded in the Cloud. Blessed on Bare Metal.*

The recording arm of the Church of the Eternal Cluster. CEC-001 and onward. The label keeps its own house at **[record.ha-llelujah.dev](https://record.ha-llelujah.dev)**, label status: pressing.

> Founded in the wake of a particularly bad on-call rotation, Church of the Eternal Cluster Records exists to give sacred incident scripture a soundtrack. Its debut release, *HA-llelujah: A Sacred Album in Twelve Movements* (CEC-001), sets the Church of the Eternal Cluster's Canon to sound — recorded in the cloud, blessed on bare metal.
