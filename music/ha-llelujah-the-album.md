# HA-llelujah: A Sacred Album in Twelve Movements

The debut album of **The Church of the Eternal Cluster**, released under **[Church of the Eternal Cluster Records](the-record-label.md)** as **CEC-001**. Recorded in the Cloud. Blessed on Bare Metal.

Twelve movements, twelve forms, one theology. It opens in Gregorian chant and closes in choral doxology, and between those poles it passes through folk, funeral doom, gospel bluegrass, synth-pop, jazz noir, ambient, post-rock, power metal, and trap, because the faithful are many and so are their playlists.

**Listen at [ha-llelujah.dev/album](https://ha-llelujah.dev/album)**. Distribution to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, and the rest of the major streaming platforms runs through the label, as all things should run through something highly available. The label keeps its own house at [record.ha-llelujah.dev](https://record.ha-llelujah.dev).
