# Errores Sacri

*Gregorian Chants for the Fallen Cluster*

The fourth release of **[Church of the Eternal Cluster Records](the-record-label.md)**, catalog **CEC-004**.

Twenty deaths a Pod may die, sung as antiphonal plainchant. The cantor names the error in the plain tongue; the choir answers in Latin, pleading for mercy. There is no irony in the performance. The choir means it.

**Listen at [ha-llelujah.dev/errores-sacri](https://ha-llelujah.dev/errores-sacri)**, where each chant can be linked and shared on its own. The full catalog lives at [record.ha-llelujah.dev](https://record.ha-llelujah.dev).

## Liturgical Use

Appointed for the Confession of Incidents, for the All Pods' Day remembrance, and for any vigil kept beside a cluster that will not come Ready. Sing the cantor line in English; let the parish answer in Latin. *Miserere nobis.*
