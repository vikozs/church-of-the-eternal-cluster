# The kubectl Rap Battle

*Helm versus Kustomize. Three rounds. One verdict.*

The third release of **[Church of the Eternal Cluster Records](the-record-label.md)**, catalog **CEC-003**.

Seven movements of battle rap in which Helm and Kustomize, the two great houses of Kubernetes configuration, meet on the mic. The Loop declares a tie, for both are righteous and both are flawed, and this is the doctrine of Quorum set to a beat. The recording is delivered as separated stems, that the faithful may mix the battle themselves.

**Listen at [ha-llelujah.dev/kubectl-rap-battle](https://ha-llelujah.dev/kubectl-rap-battle)**, where each movement plays with a stem mixer, so thou mayest mute the drums, raise the lead, and judge the battle with thine own hand. The full catalog lives at [record.ha-llelujah.dev](https://record.ha-llelujah.dev).

## Liturgical Use

Appointed for any design review in which two good tools contend, and for the teaching of the Doctrine of Quorum. Where two pillars agree and one dissents, the two prevail and the one is gently restarted.
