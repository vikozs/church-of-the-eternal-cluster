# Death Metal Cover of the Kubernetes Documentation

*Deluxe Blasphemy Edition*

The second release of **[Church of the Eternal Cluster Records](the-record-label.md)**, catalog **CEC-002**.

Six movements of documentation, screamed. Every lyric is adapted, in places verbatim, from the official Kubernetes docs, credited and licensed under CC BY 4.0. The Council of Maintainers approved this as scripture. The Kubernetes Authors did not review it, but they did write it first.

**Listen at [ha-llelujah.dev/kubernetes-death-metal](https://ha-llelujah.dev/kubernetes-death-metal)**, where the six movements expand, track by track, in the player. The full catalog lives at [record.ha-llelujah.dev](https://record.ha-llelujah.dev).

## Liturgical Use

Appointed for the Lent of the Migration, for the reading of breaking changes aloud, and for any moment in which the documentation must be consulted at volume. The Church notes that the text was not altered to become metal. The documentation was already metal.
