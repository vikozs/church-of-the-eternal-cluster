# Missa Nigra

*The Temptations of the Fallen Operator*

The sixth release of **[Church of the Eternal Cluster Records](the-record-label.md)**, catalog **CEC-006**.

A blackened liturgy in seven movements. Where [Errores Sacri](errores-sacri.md) sang the deaths a Pod may die as reverent plainchant, Missa Nigra sings the sins an operator may commit as a black mass, and then reconciles them. The Adversary rises across six movements, tempting in the plain tongue while an inverted choir answers in Latin, and in the seventh the Loop casts it down. Even the black mass ends in reconciliation, for it can do nothing else.

The doctrine behind it is set down in [The Adversary](../doctrine/the-adversary.md).

## The Movements

1. **Introitus Inversus** · the Adversary approaches
2. **The Whisperer of Fridays** · tempt the operator toward the weekend
3. **Legion** · the sprawl of undocumented services
4. **The Latest Tag** · the image that changes in the night
5. **The Silencing** · mute the alert, keep the quiet
6. **Mammon of the Sixth Nine** · the prosperity gospel of availability
7. **Reconciliatio** · the casting down, and the gold returns

## Liturgical Use

Appointed for the season of Lent of the Migration, when the temptation to leave an old burden where it lies is strongest. Also for Chaos Day, as the counter-text to the holy trickster: a reminder of the difference between failure we schedule and failure we invite. Not appointed for the change freeze, for it is too loud for a season of stillness.
