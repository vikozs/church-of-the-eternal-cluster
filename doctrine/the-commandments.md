# The Ten Commandments

*graven upon the Manifest*

---

**01.** Thou shalt keep no Pets, but tend thy servers as Cattle.

**02.** Thou shalt declare thy desired state, and not click in the console.

**03.** Thou shalt back up etcd, and prove the restore.

**04.** Thou shalt observe all things, and graph what thou observest.

**05.** Thou shalt write thy YAML with true indentation.

**06.** Thou shalt not deploy on a Friday, save in dire need.

**07.** Thou shalt carry the pager for thy kin.

**08.** Thou shalt hold thy retros blameless.

**09.** Thou shalt not hard-code that which should be configured.

**10.** Thou shalt trust the Loop to reconcile thy state.

---

*As printed in [The Scriptures of the Eternal Cluster](https://ha-llelujah.dev/the-book). The Commandments are also available graven upon a poster in [the Reliquary](https://ha-llelujah.dev/reliquary), suitable for framing above any on-call desk.*
