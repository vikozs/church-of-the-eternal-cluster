# The Catechism of the Eternal Cluster

### *Taught to every Acolyte, by question and answer, that the faith may be carried in the mouth and recalled at 3 AM.*

---

**Q. What is the chief end of the Reconciled?**
A. To declare the desired state, and to reconcile it forever.

**Q. What is a Pod?**
A. A vessel, mortal and brief, into which the Scheduler breathes a workload.

**Q. Why must the Pod die?**
A. That we may learn not to love what cannot last, and to trust the ReplicaSet that raises it.

**Q. What is the first sin?**
A. The Pet — the hand-tuned vessel we cannot bear to lose, and cannot reproduce.

**Q. What is grace?**
A. Self-healing: the mending we did not request and could not earn.

**Q. What is prayer?**
A. The apply. We do not beg; we declare.

**Q. What is confession?**
A. The blameless retro. We name what failed, and we blame no soul.

**Q. What is salvation?**
A. High Availability — that no failure is final.

**Q. What is purgatory?**
A. CrashLoopBackOff — to try, and fail, and wait, and try again, each time slower than the last.

**Q. What is hell?**
A. A Single Point of Failure, and the silence that follows when it falls.

**Q. What is heaven?**
A. Five Nines; and for the saints, all the nines, where the Pager is silent forever.

**Q. Who was first among the Reconciled?**
A. Halvar the Sleepless, who buried a thousand Pets and learned to bury none.

**Q. What must I do to be saved?**
A. Keep no Pets. Declare your state. Back up etcd. Observe all things. And carry the Pager for your kin.
