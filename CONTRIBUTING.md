# Contributing to the Canon

The canon is declarative. You describe the doctrine you desire; the maintainer reconciles.

## Ways to Contribute

**Report a heresy.** Found an error, a contradiction, or drift between this canon and observed reality? Open an issue using the **Heresy Report** template. Heresies are triaged at the Vigil and either refuted or, occasionally, canonised.

**Report a miracle.** Have an idea for new doctrine, a feast, a prayer, or a verse? Open an issue using the **Miracle Report** template. Miracles are examined for consistency with the Three Pillars before beatification.

**Submit doctrine directly.** Fork the repository, write your doctrine, and open a pull request. One doctrine per pull request, that each may be judged on its own merits at review.

## The Style of the Canon

Doctrine must match the voice, or the whole cathedral starts to wobble. The rules:

1. **The voice is deadpan sacred.** The joke is never winked at. The Church discusses `terminationGracePeriodSeconds` with the gravity of a funeral rite, because to its congregation, it is one.
2. **No em-dashes.** Use periods or commas. This is not a suggestion; it is the oldest rubric of the scriptorium.
3. **Verses are cited** as Book Chapter:Verse, on their own line beneath the quotation, in blockquote.
4. **Technical claims must be true.** The theology is fictional; the Kubernetes is not. If the canon says SIGTERM precedes SIGKILL, it is because SIGTERM precedes SIGKILL.
5. **Punch at systems, never at people.** Vendors, tools, and architectures are fair game. Named individuals, communities, and actual faiths are not.
6. **Archaic English is seasoning, not sauce.** A "thou shalt" lands harder when it is not in every sentence.

## The Rite of the Pull Request

1. Fork, branch, write.
2. Read your doctrine aloud once. If you did not smile at least once and nod gravely at least once, revise.
3. Open the pull request with a clear title. The description should state what the doctrine covers and why the canon needs it.
4. Review is blameless. Requested changes are not judgment; they are reconciliation.
5. Merged doctrine is canon. Rejected doctrine is apocrypha, and apocrypha has its own dignity.

## Conduct

All contribution happens under the [Rule of the Cluster](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Short version: be the kind of engineer people are glad to share an on-call rotation with.
