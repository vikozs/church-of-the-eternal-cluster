# The Rule of the Cluster

*A code of conduct, written plainly, because this part is not a joke.*

## The Rule

1. **Be kind.** Treat everyone in this project's spaces, issues, pull requests, discussions, with respect, regardless of experience, identity, background, or preferred ingress controller.
2. **Be blameless.** Critique doctrine, code, and ideas. Never attack the person who wrote them. "This verse drifts from canon" is review; "you clearly don't understand quorum" is not.
3. **Punch at systems, not people.** The Church parodies infrastructure culture. Contributions that mock real individuals, real communities, or real religious faiths will not be merged and may be removed.
4. **No harassment.** No slurs, no personal attacks, no sustained hostility, no unwelcome attention. Not in issues, not in PRs, not anywhere this project lives.
5. **Assume good faith, once.** Misunderstandings happen. Patterns are not misunderstandings.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in edited or deleted contributions, locked threads, or a ban from the repository, at the maintainer's discretion. Serious or repeated violations skip the warning.

To report a problem, email **vid@kosir.info**. Reports are read by a human and handled with discretion.

## Scope

This rule applies in all project spaces and in any public space where someone is representing the project.
