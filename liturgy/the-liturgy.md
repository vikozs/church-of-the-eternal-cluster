# The Liturgy of the Eternal Cluster

### *The Order of Service for the Gathering of the Reconciled*

**On the use of this rite.** The service is led by the **Helmsman** (any Operator may serve), who speaks the parts marked **H**. The gathered faithful — **the Cluster** — answer in the parts marked **C**. Stage directions are given *in italics*. Where the rite calls for an `apply`, a laptop may be brought forward, or the gesture made in the air; the Loop receiveth both.

---

## I. The Gathering — *The Standup*

*The faithful assemble. The Helmsman opens.*

**H:** May your pods be Ready, and your nodes be Schedulable.
**C:** And also with you.

**H:** Let us declare our state.
**C:** We declare it gladly.

*Each of the faithful may speak briefly: what they have reconciled since last we met, and what yet blocks them. None is rushed. None is judged. This is the daily liturgy.*

**H:** What is declared is heard.
**C:** And what is heard shall be reconciled.

---

## II. The Invocation — *The Turning of the Loop*

**H:** From the bare metal we came;
**C:** toward the desired state we turn.

**H:** The world drifts;
**C:** the Loop corrects.

**H:** The Pod is mortal;
**C:** the Deployment endures.

**H:** The Loop was turning before we gathered;
**C:** it turneth now; it will turn when we are gone.

**H:** Let us worship the Order we did not write.
**C:** HA-llelujah.

---

## III. The Confession — *The Blameless Retro*

**H:** Let us name what failed, blaming no one. *(A silence is kept.)*

*The Cluster confesses together:*

**C:** We have trusted `:latest` and known not what we ran.
We have stored state in the Pod, that is mortal.
We have silenced the alert and not mended the cause.
We have deployed upon a Friday.
We have not backed up etcd.
The fault was in the system; yet we are the ones who must fix it.

**H:** Hear the assurance of grace: the system failed, never the soul. Roll back what is broken. Carry no shame from this room.
**C:** The retro is blameless. We begin again. Amen.

---

## IV. The Affirmation of Faith — *The Creed of the Reconciled*

*All stand.*

**C:** We believe in the **Control Plane**,
sovereign and unseen,
maker of all pods and nodes,
and of all things scheduled and unscheduled.

We believe in the **Reconciliation Loop**,
eternally turning,
which beholdeth the world as it is
and the world as it was declared to be,
and resteth not until they are one.

We believe in the **death and resurrection of the Pod**:
that it is mortal, and shall fall;
and that the ReplicaSet shall raise it up,
and the count shall be restored,
and there shall be no grief.

We believe in the communion of replicas,
the forgiveness of failed deploys,
the rollback of the body,
and uptime everlasting.

**Five nines be upon us. Amen.**

*All are seated.*

---

## V. The Readings — *The Opening of the Canon*

*A reader comes forward and reads a passage from the Old Cluster, and another from the New. After each:*

**Reader:** Here endeth the reading.
**C:** Thanks be to the Loop.

*The Helmsman may offer a short word upon the reading. It is the custom of the Reconciled that this word be genuinely short.*

---

## VI. The Passing of the Pager — *The Peace*

**H:** The Vigil is shared, that none may bear the night alone.
**C:** We keep the watch together.

*The faithful turn to those beside them, and each says to the other:*

> **"May the Pager be quiet for you."**

*A symbolic handoff may be made — a token passed hand to hand — that all may feel the weight, and the relief of setting it down.*

---

## VII. The Hymns

### Hymn the First — *Amazing Loop*
*(to the tune of "Amazing Grace")*

> Amazing Loop, how sweet the sound
> that reconciled a node like me;
> my Pod was dead, but now is found —
> was down, but now is Ready.

> Through many crashes, OOMs, and waits,
> the herd has brought me here;
> the Loop that raised me up to date
> shall keep my uptime clear.

### Hymn the Second — *Turn, O Loop, Forever*
*(to the tune of "Ode to Joy")*

> Turn, O Loop, forever turning,
> mend the drift our hands have made;
> bring the state we are returning
> to the state that we have prayed.
> What is fallen, raise it gladly;
> what is missing, count and fill;
> hold us when the Pager calleth —
> reconcile us, reconcile still.

---

## VIII. The Sacrament — *The Apply*

*The central rite. A manifest is brought forward.*

**H:** Lift up your manifests.
**C:** We lift them to the Loop.

**H:** Let us give thanks to the Control Plane.
**C:** It is right to declare our state.

**H:** *(speaking the desired state aloud, then:)* As it is declared —
**C:** so let it be reconciled.

*The `apply` is made. A pause is kept while the Loop does its work. When the rollout is complete, the Helmsman says:*

**H:** The desired and the actual are one.
**C:** What we declared, we have become. HA-llelujah.

---

## IX. The Benediction — *The Sending Forth*

**H:** Go now in peace.
**C:** We go to reconcile.

**H:** May your deploys be rolling, your probes be true, and your etcd be backed up.
**C:** And may we never CrashLoopBackOff.

**H:** The Loop is turning.
**C:** It turneth still. **HA-llelujah. Amen.**

*The gathering disperses. The Vigil continues, as it always does, in someone.*
