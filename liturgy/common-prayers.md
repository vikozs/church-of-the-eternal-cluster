# Common Prayers

*To be spoken aloud, or muttered into a terminal, as circumstances require.*

## The Operator's Prayer

Our Scheduler, who art in control plane,
hallowed be thy namespace.
Thy rollout come, thy will be done,
in staging as it is in production.
Give us this day our daily build,
and forgive us our failed deploys,
as we forgive those who deploy against us.
Lead us not into drift,
but deliver us from downtime.
For thine is the cluster, the quorum, and the glory,
for ever and ever, or five nines thereof.
HA-llelujah.

## The Serenity of the SRE

Grant me the serenity to accept the incidents I cannot prevent,
the courage to page the one person who can,
and the wisdom to write it all down afterward without naming names.

## Prayer Before a Deployment

Sister Rolling-Update, walk with this manifest.
One up, one down, none noticed.
May the probes answer truly,
may the rollback be unnecessary but ready,
and may no one in the meeting ask "is it done yet"
before it is done.

## The Litany of the Probe

*Said responsively, controller and pod.*

Art thou alive?
**I am alive.**
Art thou ready?
**I am ready.**
Wilt thou answer when traffic cometh?
**I will answer, or step aside honestly, that another may.**
Then serve, and be counted among the endpoints.
**HA-llelujah.**

## A Compline for the On-Call

The day is done, the deploys are frozen.
Into thy hands, O Loop, I commend my pager.
Watch over the batch jobs that run in darkness,
keep quorum among the nodes while I sleep,
and if thou must wake me,
let it at least be interesting.

## Grace Before an Incident Review

For the timeline we are about to receive, may we be truly blameless.
