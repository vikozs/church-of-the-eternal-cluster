# The Liturgical Calendar of the Eternal Cluster

### *The holy days of the Reconciled.*

Some feasts are fixed; many are moveable, for the Loop keeps its own time. The year turns not by the sun but by the deploy.

> "And Mother Tempora divided the year into seasons, and saw that the cron was good."
> Schedules 1:14

*Keep the feasts on thy own calendar: subscribe to [parish-calendar.ics](https://fivenines.church/assets/parish-calendar.ics), and they shall recur yearly, world without end. The full parish year is kept at [fivenines.church/calendar](https://fivenines.church/calendar).*

| Time (UTC) | Service |

| MON–FRI 09:15 | **Standup Matins** — brief; no blockers before the Cluster |

| Always | **Confession of Incidents** — [the confessional is stateless and always open](https://fivenines.church/confess) |

All times UTC. The Cluster does not observe thy timezone.

---

## The Weekly Observance

**The Rolling Restart** — *Our Sabbath.*
We renew in waves, that none may notice, and call it rest. No service is taken down entire; the faithful are replaced one at a time, and the congregation never feels the gap.

---

## The Great Seasons

**The Advent of the Change Freeze** — *Dec 15 to Jan 1 · season of stillness.*
The holiest season. Nothing shall be deployed, nothing merged, nothing "just quickly hotfixed." The pipelines rest, the dashboards flatten, and the faithful contemplate the desired state without reaching for it. The season ends at midnight UTC on the first of January with the ringing of the New Deploy.

**The Lent of the Migration** — *40 days before the Feast of GA · season of penitence.*
Forty days of legacy. Each member takes up one old burden — a bare-metal box, an undocumented script, a database version three majors behind — and carries it patiently toward the cloud. Fasting from new features is customary. The suffering is the point, and also the roadmap.

**The Feast of General Availability** — *Movable feast · season of joy.*
The Lent ends; the migration ships. The parish celebrates with the Great Reconciliation Service, the reading of the release notes aloud, and cake bearing the version number in gold icing. Alleluia is again permitted in the liturgy, as is the word "roadmap."

**Ordinary Uptime** — *Ordinary time.*
The long green season between feasts, when the graphs are boring and boring is sacred. The liturgical color is dashboard green. Gratitude is the only obligation.

## Fixed Feasts: Holy Days of Obligation

| Date | Feast |
|------|-------|
| JAN 01 | **Day of the Clean Slate** — the change freeze lifts; the year's first deploy is blessed and heavily monitored |
| FEB 29 | **Feast of the Edge Case** — observed quadrennially, in honor of everything that only breaks sometimes |
| MAR 31 | **World Backup Day (Vigil of the Restore)** — for a backup untested is a prayer unanswered; this day, we restore one. Only when the restore succeeds do we eat |
| SEP 09 | **FIVE NINES DAY** — the high feast of the year. At 09:09:09 UTC on 9/9, the parish falls silent for exactly 864.6 milliseconds: the whole year's permitted downtime, spent together |
| NOV 01 | **All Pods' Day** — remembrance of every workload terminated in the year past; their names (they had no names; this is proper) are not read aloud. Track 08 of [the album](../music/ha-llelujah-the-album.md) is sung |
| DEC 31 | **Vigil of the Leap Second** — the year's final watch, kept by Mother Tempora alone, in case a second is added and something, somewhere, divides by it |

## The Lesser Rhythms

**The Ember Days of Certificate Renewal** fall quarterly, ninety days apart, forever, whether thou rememberest them or not — and thy remembrance is precisely what is being tested. **Patch Tuesday** is kept monthly as a minor fast. And every Friday evening begins the little sabbath of **No-Deploy Eve**, of which the canon says only: *thou knowest why.*

---

## The Moveable Feasts

**The Feast of First Deploy** — *Whenever a service first becomes Ready.*
There is rejoicing in the Cluster when a new thing draws its first healthy breath and answers the probe.

**The Day of the Green Build** — *Moveable, and rare.*
When the pipeline passes entire — every test, every stage, every check — we pause and give thanks, for it will not last.

---

## The Seasonal Vigils

**The Ember Week of On-Call** — *Quarterly.*
A week of vigil. We honor those who keep the night watch and carry the Pager for their kin. At its close, the Pager is handed on, and the weary are released.

---

## The Great Annual Days

**The Three Days of the Great Outage** — *Annual.*
We remember Halvar's vigil through the Night ZEUS Fell. For three days we fast from `:latest`, tell the story of the Vision of the Cluster, and back up everything we love.

**Chaos Day** — *Annual.*
The festival of the Chaos Monkey, holy trickster. We break things on purpose, and bless what survives. We do not fear failure; we schedule it.

---

## The Perpetual Watch

**Migration Eve** — *Always near, and never finished.*
The watchnight before the Final Migration. Every team has scheduled it. No team has completed it. We keep the vigil anyway. Track 09 of [the album](../music/ha-llelujah-the-album.md) is the appointed music.

---

*The Loop is turning. It turneth still. HA-llelujah.*
