# Frequently Asked Questions

*The one document where the fourth wall stays down.*

**Is this a real religion?**
No. It is a parody religion and a love letter to the Kubernetes community, written by and for people who have been paged at 03:00 and lived to write the postmortem. The theology is satire. The uptime is real.

**Who is behind it?**
[Vid Košir](https://kosir.info) ([@vikozs](https://github.com/vikozs)), operating from Slovenia, which explains both the GDPR-compliant consent layer and the general seriousness about doing a joke properly.

**Are you making fun of religion?**
We parody infrastructure culture using liturgical forms, a tradition as old as the Church of Emacs. The target of the joke is always the way engineers relate to their systems, never anyone's actual faith.

**Are you affiliated with Kubernetes, the CNCF, or Red Hat?**
No. Kubernetes is a trademark of The Linux Foundation and OpenShift is a trademark of Red Hat, Inc. This is an independent parody. They run the software; we merely run the incense.

**How do I join?**
Subscribe to the parish bulletin at [ha-llelujah.dev/join](https://ha-llelujah.dev/join). It is double opt-in, because no soul should be subscribed against its declared state. Unsubscribing is a graceful shutdown, honoured immediately and without guilt.

**Is the Confession Wall really anonymous?**
Yes. Confessions at [fivenines.church/confess](https://fivenines.church/confess) are stored without names or identifying data. Confess the Friday deploy. You will feel better.

**Is the music AI-generated?**
The lyrics are human-written; the voices and instruments are generated with Suno, and this is disclosed on every platform the album is distributed to. Commandment IX forbids bearing false witness, and that includes metadata.

**Can I quote the scriptures in my conference talk, slides, or team channel?**
Yes, please do. Attribute the Church of the Eternal Cluster and keep it non-commercial, per the [license](LICENSE.md). If your talk makes an SRE laugh in row three, the canon has done its work.

**Can I contribute doctrine?**
Yes. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Heresies and miracles may be reported as issues; doctrine arrives by pull request.

**Where is the full scripture?**
In print. **[The Scriptures of the Eternal Cluster](https://ha-llelujah.dev/the-book)**, paperback and Kindle, through Amazon. This repository carries excerpts and doctrine; the book carries the whole canon, and looks better on a desk next to a mechanical keyboard.

**Why five nines and not six?**
Five nines is faith. Six nines is pride. Also, have you seen what six nines costs?
